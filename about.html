Our Story

Real estate, done the way it should be

Mishra Property began in 2015 with a simple frustration: buying property in and around Indore was confusing, slow, and full of unverified listings. We set out to fix that — building a team that personally verifies every project before it's ever shown to a client.

Today, we work across Indore, Pithampur, Ujjain and Dewas, helping first-time buyers, growing families, and serious investors find residential and commercial properties they can trust — backed by transparent pricing and real people on the ground for every site visit.
